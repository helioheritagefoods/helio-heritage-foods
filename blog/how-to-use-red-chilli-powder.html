How to Use Red Chilli Powder in Indian Cooking

Red chilli powder brings heat and colour to many Indian dishes. Chilli varieties differ in pungency, so start with a small amount and adjust to your taste.

1. Add to a masala base

Use chilli powder in onion, tomato or other recipe masala bases. Add with other ground spices and stir briefly, taking care not to burn it.

2. Season dals and curries

Stir a little chilli powder into the cooking base or tempering, then adjust after tasting. You can always add more, but it is difficult to reduce heat once added.

3. Use in marinades

Mix chilli powder with ingredients such as yoghurt, ginger, garlic and other seasonings as a marinade for vegetables, paneer or other foods.

4. Choose colour and heat thoughtfully

Chilli powders can vary in colour and pungency. Read product information and choose according to the flavour and heat level you want in the finished dish.

5. Store it carefully

Keep chilli powder sealed in a clean, dry container away from sunlight, heat and moisture. Use a dry spoon and avoid storing it near steam.
