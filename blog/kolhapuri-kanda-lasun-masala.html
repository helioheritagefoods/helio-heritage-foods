Kolhapuri Kanda Lasun Masala: Uses & Cooking Tips

Kolhapuri kanda lasun masala is a bold regional spice blend associated with Maharashtrian cooking. Depending on the recipe, it may combine chilli, onion, garlic and a mix of aromatic spices to give gravies, curries and vegetable dishes a distinctive flavour.

What dishes can use kanda lasun masala?

  • Usal and matki: add gradually to the gravy or rassa and adjust to taste.
  • Vegetable curries: use in the cooking base for potato, brinjal or mixed vegetables.
  • Rassa-style gravies: stir into the base and simmer so the flavours blend.
  • Marinades: combine with a suitable base and other seasonings for paneer or vegetables.

How to use it without overpowering a dish

Start with a small amount, cook it briefly in the masala base as your recipe directs, then taste and adjust. Blend in liquid as needed; avoid scorching ground spices. The heat and salt levels vary by blend, so check the product information and adjust the rest of the seasoning.

How is it different from everyday spice powders?

Single spices such as turmeric, chilli and coriander powder are usually used individually or in a simple combination. A regional blend brings several flavour elements together, so it can be a convenient starting point for a dish rather than a single seasoning.

Frequently asked questions

What is Kolhapuri kanda lasun masala?

It is a regional Maharashtrian spice blend commonly associated with chilli, onion, garlic and other spices. Ingredients and proportions vary between recipes and producers.

Which dishes can I make with it?

It can be used in usal, rassa, vegetable curries and other dishes where a bold, savoury flavour is desired. Adjust the amount to suit your recipe and taste.

Is it very spicy?

Heat varies depending on the recipe and chilli varieties used. Begin with a small quantity and add more gradually.
