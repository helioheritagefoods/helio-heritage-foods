A Practical Guide to Kolhapuri Tambda Rassa

Learn a practical home-style Tambda Rassa method, flavour-building steps and how to adjust heat.

In this guide
  • Practical guide
  • Related reading

Tambda rassa is a red, spicy Maharashtrian-style curry. Recipes differ across homes; this guide is a flexible home-style method, not a single definitive traditional recipe.

Starter quantities for 4 servings

  • 500 g chicken or suitable alternative
  • 2 medium onions, finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2–3 tbsp oil
  • 1–2 tsp chilli powder, adjusted to taste
  • 2 tsp coriander powder
  • ¼ tsp turmeric
  • Salt, water and a suitable regional masala to taste

Method

  1. Cook onions slowly until golden, not burnt.
  2. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook until its raw aroma fades.
  3. Lower the heat. Add powdered spices and stir briefly; add a splash of water if needed.
  4. Add the main ingredient and coat well.
  5. Add water, simmer until cooked through and season to taste.
  6. Rest briefly before serving.

Adjust the rassa

For more colour, choose a colour-focused chilli powder; for more heat, increase pungent chilli cautiously. Add water to adjust consistency. Use safe cooking practices and ensure meat is fully cooked.
