Garam Masala vs Kolhapuri Masala: How They Differ

Compare the purposes of garam masala and Kolhapuri-style masalas, their flavour profiles and how to use them.

In this guide
  • Practical guide
  • Related reading

These blends are not interchangeable by definition. Recipes, ingredient lists and heat levels vary widely by maker and region.

Garam masala

Often built around warm aromatic spices such as cinnamon, cloves, cardamom and other ingredients. It may be used near the end of cooking or within a recipe's spice base.

Kolhapuri-style masala

Regional blends can be more chilli-forward, roasted or complex, and may be used as a core seasoning in rassa, usal and vegetable dishes.

How to substitute

Check the blend ingredients. If substituting, begin with a smaller amount, adjust chilli and salt separately, and taste. The flavour and heat may differ substantially.

Cooking tip

Ground blends can scorch quickly. Add at a controlled heat, stir and use a splash of liquid when necessary.
