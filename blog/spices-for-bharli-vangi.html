Spices for Bharli Vangi: A Balanced Flavour Guide

Learn how roasted peanut, sesame, coconut and spice flavours can work in bharli vangi and how to adjust the stuffing.

In this guide
  • Practical guide
  • Related reading

Bharli vangi (stuffed eggplant) varies from family to family. Many versions combine peanuts, sesame, coconut, onion and spices for a thick stuffing and gravy.

Starter stuffing for 4 servings

  • 8 small eggplants, slit and prepared
  • ½ cup roasted peanuts, ground
  • 1–2 tbsp sesame, roasted and ground
  • 2 tbsp coconut, optional
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 1–2 tsp coriander powder
  • Chilli powder, turmeric and regional masala to taste
  • Oil, salt and water

Method

  1. Combine dry ingredients and seasoning, then add a little water to make a moist stuffing.
  2. Fill eggplants carefully and reserve extra stuffing.
  3. Heat oil, cook onion and add the remaining stuffing with a splash of water.
  4. Arrange eggplants, cover and cook gently, turning as needed.
  5. Add water gradually for gravy; cook until eggplants are tender.

Peanut and sesame amounts can be adjusted for texture. Ensure eggplants are cooked through and avoid scorching the stuffing.
