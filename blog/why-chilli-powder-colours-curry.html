Why Chilli Powder Changes the Colour of Your Curry

Explore the role of chilli variety, fat, heat, cooking time and other ingredients in curry colour.

In this guide
  • Practical guide
  • Related reading

Chilli powder contributes pigments, but the final colour depends on variety, freshness, quantity, fat, heat and other ingredients in the pot.

Factors that affect colour

  • Variety: different chillies have different pigment and heat profiles.
  • Quantity: more powder may intensify colour but also alter flavour and heat.
  • Fat: pigments can disperse differently in oil than in water.
  • Heat: scorching can darken the base and create bitter notes.
  • Other ingredients: tomato, onion, turmeric and liquids change the final appearance.

How to keep colour without burning

Lower the heat before adding chilli powder. Stir briefly, add liquid if needed and cook gently. Avoid using colour alone as a purity or safety test.

Kitchen takeaway: Use a consistent measured method, adjust slowly and record what works for your own kitchen.
