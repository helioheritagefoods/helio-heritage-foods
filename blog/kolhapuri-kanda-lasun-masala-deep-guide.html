Kolhapuri Kanda Lasun Masala: A Deeper Kitchen Guide

Explore how kanda lasun masala works in usal, rassa, vegetables and marinades, with adjustment tips.

In this guide
  • Practical guide
  • Related reading

Kanda-lasun masala is a regional-style blend whose recipes and intensity vary. Onion, garlic, chilli and other spices may contribute savoury, pungent and roasted notes, depending on the blend.

Where it works

  • Usal and sprouted legumes: add to the simmering base.
  • Rassa: use in the masala base, then adjust liquid.
  • Vegetables: add with aromatics and cook gently.
  • Marinades: combine with an appropriate wet base; refrigerate foods that need refrigeration.

How to use it

Begin with about 1–2 teaspoons for a family-sized dish, recognizing that blend strength differs. Taste and add more as needed. If it contains salt, account for that when seasoning.

Keep it from turning bitter

Use moderate heat, stir and add liquid if the mixture begins sticking. Avoid dry roasting an already-ground blend for too long.

What to pair with it

Try it with legumes, potatoes, eggplant or other vegetables, adjusting chilli, salt and liquid to suit the dish.
