Matki Usal: When to Add Each Spice

A practical guide to building flavour in matki usal with a clear spice sequence and adjustable quantities.

In this guide
  • Practical guide
  • Related reading

In matki usal, the sequence of cooking aromatics and spices helps build flavour while keeping powders from scorching.

For roughly 4 servings

  • 2 cups cooked sprouted matki
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 tomato, optional
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • ¼ tsp turmeric
  • 1–2 tsp coriander powder
  • Chilli powder and regional masala to taste
  • Oil, mustard seeds, cumin, curry leaves, salt and water

Sequence

  1. Heat oil and temper mustard seeds, cumin and curry leaves.
  2. Add onion and cook until softened and lightly golden.
  3. Add ginger-garlic paste; cook briefly.
  4. Add tomato if using and cook down.
  5. Reduce heat and stir in turmeric, coriander and chilli powders; avoid burning.
  6. Add cooked matki, water and salt; simmer to desired consistency.
  7. Finish with regional masala, taste and adjust.

Sprouts and cooking times vary. Ensure matki is cooked to your preferred safe tenderness and adjust spice amounts gradually.

Kitchen takeaway: Treat these as starting points; ingredients, recipe and batch vary. Measure, taste and adjust carefully.
