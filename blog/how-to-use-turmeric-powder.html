How to Use Turmeric Powder in Everyday Indian Cooking

Turmeric powder is a familiar ingredient in many Indian kitchens. Its earthy aroma and warm colour make it a useful part of everyday cooking, from simple dal to vegetable dishes and marinades. Here are practical ways to use it.

1. Add it to dal and lentils

Add a small amount of turmeric while cooking dal, lentils or beans. It blends into the cooking liquid and gives the dish its characteristic golden colour. Adjust the quantity to your recipe and taste.

2. Use it in vegetable dishes

Turmeric is often added near the beginning of a vegetable preparation, alongside oil and other seasonings. Stir briefly and avoid scorching the spices before adding vegetables or liquid.

3. Season rice and khichdi

A pinch of turmeric can colour plain rice, pulao or khichdi. Start with a modest amount so it complements rather than dominates the other flavours.

4. Include it in marinades

Combine turmeric with other spices, salt and a suitable marinade base for vegetables, paneer or other ingredients. Follow your recipe and refrigerate ingredients that require chilled storage.

5. Store turmeric powder carefully

  • Keep the powder in a clean, dry, airtight container.
  • Store away from moisture, heat and direct sunlight.
  • Use a dry spoon and close the container promptly.

How much should you use?

Recipes vary. Begin with a small quantity—often a pinch or roughly a quarter teaspoon in a modest family-sized preparation—and adjust to your preference. Too much can make a dish taste bitter or overly earthy.
