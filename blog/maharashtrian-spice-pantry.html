A Guide to Common Maharashtrian Kitchen Spices

A practical guide to the everyday spices used across Maharashtrian kitchens, with notes on their roles and regional variation.

In this guide
  • Practical guide
  • Related reading

Maharashtrian cooking is diverse, with household and regional differences. This pantry guide introduces common ingredients and their roles rather than defining one fixed recipe.

Everyday spices

  • Halad (turmeric): earthy notes and colour.
  • Mirchi (chilli): heat and colour; variety matters.
  • Dhania (coriander): rounded, aromatic flavour.
  • Jeera (cumin): warm aroma, often used in tempering.
  • Mohri (mustard): characteristic tempering aroma.
  • Hing (asafoetida): concentrated savoury aroma, used sparingly.
  • Garam masala and regional blends: recipe-dependent aromatic or bold flavour.

Regional blends

Goda masala, kala masala, kanda-lasun masala and other blends have varied formulations. Learn the ingredients from the maker or recipe, and adjust to the dish.

Keep your spice shelf practical

Store powders sealed and dry. Buy amounts suited to your usage, and keep a note of the blends your family enjoys.
