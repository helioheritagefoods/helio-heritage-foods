How to Use Coriander Powder in Indian Cooking

Coriander powder, made from coriander seeds, is a familiar ingredient in Indian cooking. Its warm, gently citrus-like aroma adds depth to everyday dishes.

1. Use in vegetable curries

Add coriander powder to the spice base when preparing vegetables, curries or gravies. Stir with the other seasonings and a little liquid as your recipe directs.

2. Build flavour in dal and gravy

A modest amount can round out the flavour of lentils, dals and gravy dishes. Add gradually and taste as you cook.

3. Season marinades

Combine coriander powder with other spices, ginger, garlic, yoghurt or a suitable marinade base for vegetables and paneer.

4. Blend with other spices

Coriander works alongside cumin, chilli, turmeric and other spices in many home-style masala combinations. Proportions depend on the recipe and personal preference.

5. Keep aroma fresh

Store powder in a tightly sealed container in a cool, dry place, away from direct sunlight, heat and moisture. Use a dry spoon.
