Spices for everyday cooking

  • Turmeric powder (Halad): for dals, vegetables, rice and everyday recipes.
  • Red chilli powder (Tikhat): add colour and heat to curries, gravies and regional dishes; choose quantity to suit your taste.
  • Coriander powder (Dhanepud): a fragrant staple for curries, masala bases and vegetable preparations.
  • Cumin powder (Jeera): for raita, chaas, jeera rice and seasoning.
  • Garam masala: aromatic finishing and cooking blend for many Indian dishes.
  • Kolhapuri spice blend: regional-inspired flavour; enquire about availability and pack sizes.
